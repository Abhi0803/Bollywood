# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 24 May 2026**
**Last updated: 24 May 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what data Naam Bolo (the "App") collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. The App is operated by Abhinav Jha ("we", "us", "our").

## TL;DR

Naam Bolo is a party game. It does not collect personal data on a server. All your game data lives on your phone. The only external connections the App makes are to Apple's iTunes Search API (to find song previews) and to Apple Music (when you tap "Open in Apple Music").

## 1. Data we collect

### 1.1 Data stored only on your device

The following information is saved on your iPhone or iPad in the App's local storage and **never transmitted to us or any third party**:

- **Team names, colors, and emojis** you set up
- **Filter preferences** (era, mood, rounds, timer, hints, difficulty)
- **Recently-played song IDs** (so the picker doesn't repeat songs)
- **Blocked song IDs** (songs you've marked "never play")
- **User-added songs** to your personal catalog
- **Game history** (which songs played in which rounds, which team won)

You can erase all of this at any time by deleting and reinstalling the App.

### 1.2 Data sent to Apple

The App makes the following network requests directly to Apple's servers. We do not see, log, or store the contents of these requests.

- **iTunes Search API** (`itunes.apple.com/search`): the App sends a search query (e.g. "Tum Hi Ho Aashiqui 2") and receives a list of matching tracks with public 30-second preview URLs. No personal identifiers are attached.
- **Apple Music preview streaming** (Apple CDN): when a round starts, the App streams a 30-second `.m4a` preview directly from Apple's CDN.
- **Apple Music deep link**: when you tap "Open in Apple Music" on the Reveal screen, your phone opens Apple's Music app with the relevant song. This is a system-level link and Apple's own Privacy Policy applies from that point.

These requests are governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### 1.3 Data we do NOT collect

The App does **not** collect or process:

- Your name, email, phone number, address, or any other personal identifier
- Your location
- Your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
- Your IP address (we operate no backend)
- Any analytics, crash reports, or telemetry
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any tracking signals

The App does not contain any third-party SDKs that collect data.

## 2. Account and authentication

The current version of the App does not require sign-in to play. Future versions may offer optional account creation through Apple's "Sign in with Apple" or similar providers. If you opt in to such an account in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe what is collected and how to delete it.

## 3. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from any user. If you are under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), please ensure a parent or guardian is aware that you are using the App. Because we do not collect personal data, we cannot identify or delete data belonging to a specific child user; uninstalling the App removes all on-device data.

## 4. Music content

Songs played in the App are 30-second preview clips streamed from Apple's iTunes Store, made publicly available by Apple for any application to use. The App does not store, cache, redistribute, or modify these clips. Song metadata (title, movie, year, director, cast) is hand-curated by the App's developer and contributors. Copyright in the underlying recordings belongs to the respective rights-holders.

## 5. Your choices

- **Stop using the App**: uninstall to remove all locally-stored data.
- **Block a song**: tap "Bad song" during gameplay; that song's ID is added to a local blocklist.
- **Clear preferences**: in iOS Settings, find Naam Bolo and use "Reset" or simply reinstall the App.
- **Disable network access**: in iOS Settings, turn off Naam Bolo's "Local Network" / "Mobile Data" permissions; the App will run with no audio (preview URLs cannot be fetched).

## 6. Security

Because the App stores all data only on your device and operates no backend, the security of your data depends on the security of your device. We recommend keeping iOS up to date and using a passcode.

## 7. International users

The App is developed in India. If you use the App from outside India, you understand that the App's design and these terms are based on Indian law and that any disputes will be governed accordingly (see Terms of Service).

## 8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the App's "About" section and the "Effective date" above will be updated. Continued use of the App after a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
