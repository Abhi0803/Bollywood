# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 29 May 2026**
**Last updated: 29 May 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what data Naam Bolo (the "App") collects, how it is used, your choices, and how to delete your data. The App is operated by Abhinav Jha ("we", "us", "our").

## TL;DR

Naam Bolo is a Bollywood song-guessing party game. You can play it **without an account** by tapping "Skip · Play as guest" on the Login screen — in which case the only data we touch is on-device game state. If you choose to sign in (Apple, Google, or email), we store a minimal account record so your scores can sync; you can **delete that account from inside the App at any time** (Settings → Delete account).

## 1. Data we collect

### 1.1 Data stored only on your device

The following information is saved on your iPhone or iPad in the App's local storage and **never transmitted to us or any third party**:

- Team names, colors, and emojis you set up
- Filter preferences (era, mood, rounds, timer, hints, difficulty)
- Recently-played and blocked song IDs
- User-added songs in your personal catalog
- Game history (which songs played in which rounds, which team won)
- Whether you connected Apple Music in Settings

You can erase all of this at any time by deleting and reinstalling the App.

### 1.2 Data we collect when you sign in (optional)

Sign-in is **optional**. If you skip sign-in (guest mode), this section does not apply to you. If you sign in via "Continue with Apple", "Continue with Google", or email OTP, our authentication provider stores:

- Your **email address** (used as your account identifier)
- Your **display name** (only if you grant it during Apple / Google sign-in)
- An opaque user ID generated by the provider
- Timestamps of account creation and last sign-in

Authentication is handled by **Supabase** (https://supabase.com), running on AWS infrastructure in Mumbai (ap-south-1). Email delivery for the magic-link / OTP code uses **Resend** (https://resend.com).

We do **not** collect: your password (passwords are managed by Apple, Google, or Supabase respectively), your contacts, your photos, your location, your IP address beyond what's necessary for one-time auth requests, or any advertising identifiers.

### 1.3 Diagnostic and analytics data

To keep the App reliable and understand what features people use, we collect a limited amount of telemetry. None of it is sold or shared with advertisers.

- **Crash reports (Sentry, https://sentry.io)**: when the App crashes, we receive a stack trace, the iOS version, device model, and an anonymous session ID. We do not log your email, name, sign-in tokens, or any game content. We drop iOS "WatchdogTermination" events at the SDK before they leave your device.
- **Product analytics (PostHog, https://posthog.com)**: we record which screens you view, which game events fire (round started, song reported, etc.), and aggregate timing data. If you are signed in, your email is sent so events can be associated to your account (PostHog calls this "identify"); if you sign out or delete your account, that identifier is reset and future events are anonymous again.
- **Apple Music connection events**: we record whether you connected Apple Music and whether playback succeeded, but never the songs you played outside Naam Bolo.

You can disable network access for the App in iOS Settings to stop all of this at the cost of also disabling audio previews.

### 1.4 Music content

- **30-second previews** are streamed from Apple's iTunes Search API (https://itunes.apple.com/search) directly to your device. We do not proxy, cache, or store any audio. iTunes Search API is Apple's own free public API and Apple's [Affiliate Program ToS](https://www.apple.com/legal/itunesmusic/services/) explicitly permits preview playback by third-party apps.
- **Full songs (Apple Music)** are played, if you opt in, via Apple's official **MusicKit** framework using **your** Apple Music subscription. The framework streams from Apple directly; the App does not handle, store, or transmit the audio.
- **Apple Music deep links** — when you tap "Open in Apple Music" on the Reveal screen, your phone opens Apple's Music app. Apple's Privacy Policy applies from that point.
- **Song catalog** (titles, movies, years, directors, cast hints) is hand-curated metadata shipped inside the App. We never transmit audio files.

## 2. Account deletion

**You can delete your account and all server-side data from inside the App, at any time, without contacting us.**

How to delete:

1. Open Naam Bolo
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Tap **Delete account**
4. Confirm in the dialog

This:
- Permanently deletes your account row from our authentication database (Supabase)
- Signs you out on this device
- Wipes locally-stored game state on this device
- Resets your PostHog identifier (future analytics events from this device are anonymous)

Crash reports already received (Sentry) are retained for up to **90 days** under Sentry's default retention; they contain no personally-identifying data tied to your account.

If you cannot delete in-app for any reason, email **jhaabhinav08@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my Naam Bolo account" and we will delete it within 7 days.

## 3. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you are under that age, please do not sign in; play in guest mode so that no personal data is collected. If a parent or guardian believes a child has signed in, contact us at jhaabhinav08@gmail.com and we will delete the account within 7 days.

## 4. Your choices

- **Stop using the App**: uninstall to remove all on-device data.
- **Use guest mode**: skip sign-in so no account is created.
- **Delete your account**: Settings → Delete account (see Section 2).
- **Block a song**: tap "Bad song" during gameplay; song ID is added to a local blocklist.
- **Disable Apple Music access**: iOS Settings → Naam Bolo → toggle off Apple Music.
- **Disable network access**: iOS Settings → Naam Bolo → turn off "Local Network" / "Mobile Data"; audio previews stop working.

## 5. Security

Authentication tokens are stored on-device in Apple's iOS Keychain (via `expo-secure-store`). Network requests to our authentication and analytics providers use HTTPS. Supabase enforces row-level security on user records so one signed-in user can never read or modify another's data.
