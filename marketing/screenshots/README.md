# Screenshots

Drop iPhone screenshots here. AirDrop from your iPhone to your Mac/PC, or use
iCloud Photos, then drag into this folder.

## Naming convention

Use `NN-name.png` so they sort in display order:

```
01-splash.png
02-login.png
03-home.png
04-teams.png
05-filters.png
06-playing.png
07-hints.png
08-reveal.png
```

## Target devices

App Store requires screenshots for:
- **6.7" iPhone** (e.g. iPhone 15/16 Pro Max) — **1290 × 2796**
- **6.5" iPhone** (e.g. iPhone 11 Pro Max) — **1242 × 2688**

Take screenshots on whatever iPhone you have. If your iPhone is smaller, we can
upscale or take fresh screenshots on a simulator later.

## What to capture

See `APP_STORE_LISTING.md` (in repo root) for the full list. Summary:

1. **Splash** — kill & reopen, screenshot the brand moment
2. **Login** — sign-in screen with all three auth buttons
3. **Home** — after signing in
4. **Teams setup** — 2 teams with names + colors
5. **Filters** — Era + Mood chips selected
6. **Playing** — vinyl spinning, timer ~0:20
7. **Hints overlay** — hints panel open
8. **Reveal** — song revealed with movie name + scores

## Pre-screenshot setup

- Sign out so login screen is fresh
- Charge to ≥80% (looks better in status bar)
- Enable Do Not Disturb (no notifications)
- Use realistic team names ("The Khans" / "The Kapoors")
